Findlay Lodge No. 227 is the Blue Lodge of the Findlay Masonic Complex. Here a man receives the three degrees of Entered Apprentice, Fellowcraft and Master Mason, and every member of the other bodies in this building began in a Blue Lodge.

We meet on the second Tuesday of each month and share dinner first. The rest of the year goes to degree work, the pancake breakfasts and the lodge's scholarship for Hancock County students.

B. Temple

A warmer, traditional direction: Source Serif 4 at 18px, navy brand with gold ornament on an ivory canvas, centred masthead, and the building photograph used once on the home page, with no overlay and no text on it.

Body identity on the calendar comes from each body's own emblem, which already carries its traditional colour. The UI adds no per-body colours; gold is ornament only.

C. Noticeboard

Calendar first: the home page opens on this week at the Temple, and each body page opens on its next meeting. Atkinson Hyperlegible Next at 18px, sentence-case labels, neutral ink brand.

The single-accent rule bends for body identity only: six per-body colours appear as a swatch, bar or date tile, always next to the body's name, on calendar entries, meeting cards and body pages. Nothing else on the site is coloured.
